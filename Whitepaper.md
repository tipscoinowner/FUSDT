# FUSDT – Whitepaper 

---

## 1. Introduction

FUSDT is a fully reserved, multisig‑controlled USD stablecoin designed for institutional adoption, transparency, and long‑term stability.  
It operates on both EVM and TRON networks, providing interoperability and broad ecosystem compatibility.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Existing stablecoins often suffer from:
- Opaque reserve structures  
- Centralized control without transparent governance  
- Complex or algorithmic mechanisms that introduce systemic risk  

FUSDT aims to address these issues with a simple, fully backed, and governance‑driven model.

---

## 3. Design Principles

- Full reserve backing  
- Multisig governance  
- Minimal, audit‑friendly contracts  
- Transparent documentation  
- Exchange and DeFi compatibility  

---

## 4. Architecture

### 4.1 Smart Contracts

- ERC20 contract on EVM  
- TRC20 contract on TRON  
- Multisig‑controlled mint/burn  
- Pause/Unpause security mechanism  

### 4.2 Governance

- Multisig threshold (2/3 or 3/5)  
- Policy‑driven mint/burn  
- Reserve proof publication schedule  

---

## 5. Reserve Model

- 1:1 USD‑denominated reserves  
- Held in regulated financial institutions  
- Periodic reserve attestations  
- Public reserve proof documentation  

---

## 6. Risk Management

- No algorithmic peg  
- No under‑collateralization  
- Strict access control  
- Security audits  
- Continuous monitoring  

---

## 7. Use Cases

- On‑chain settlement  
- DeFi collateral  
- Exchange base pair  
- Cross‑border payments  

---

## 8. Roadmap

- Phase 1: Testnet deployments (EVM + TRON)  
- Phase 2: Mainnet launch  
- Phase 3: Exchange listings  
- Phase 4: Institutional integrations  

---

## 9. Conclusion

FUSDT is designed to be a transparent, secure, and institution‑grade stablecoin, setting a higher standard for reserve‑backed digital assets.
